## Methods

Round-robin over the three D-014 agents with `hive-arena`: paired
colour-swapped games from common seeded random openings (4 plies),
`--depth 1` limits (B-HEU = greedy depth 1; B-MCTS = exactly 6400 sims via
`--sims`; B-RND ignores the limit), 100 games per pairing, referee-validated
moves, 300-ply cap with truncations excluded from the score and reported
separately (arena change of commit 156bc5e, per D-008). Search verification
(task 4) ran first: tactical suite 5/5 at 400/1600/6400 sims; sign and
alternation tests green (hive-eval identity, hive-search mate signs,
hive-mcts root values, both colours).

## Limits / confounders

- The arena CI treats games as independent; openings are paired and
  colour-swapped, so the true interval is somewhat narrower — fine for
  characterisation, not for study conclusions (protocol §6 governs those).
- Single machine; B-MCTS cost figures machine-specific by design.
- 100 games/pairing = pilot volume (plan ch. 7 proposal); intervals are
  ±~5 pp on scores near 40–60%.

## Interpretation

- **Task 2 acceptance holds:** the documented heuristic dominates
  legal-random 100–0 (nothing to diagnose).
- **Ordering finding (task 5, discussed, not retuned):** B-MCTS(6400) sits
  *below* B-HEU (37.5%, −89 Elo). Diagnosis: with uniform priors, 6400
  sims spread over Hive's ~60-move branching give an effectively shallow
  search, while B-HEU's depth-1 argmax + queen-targeting quiescence is
  tactically sharp; the tanh(eval/400) leaf value also saturates on
  queen-danger positions, flattening the value signal. The 4×-budget probe
  (56.2%) confirms budget scaling, supporting this explanation. Echoes the
  literature: AZ-Hive's trained engines also lost to plain search
  (de Goede et al. 2022); strong heuristic minimax is hard to beat in Hive
  (Kampert et al. 2021).
- **Population reading:** R is a clean floor; H and M bracket a mid band
  ~90 Elo apart with different styles (sharp greedy tactics vs sampled
  search). That is a usable, honest evaluation population; whether B-MCTS
  freezes at 6400 (characterised, 27 ms/decision) or 25,600 (≈parity,
  110 ms/decision, would need re-characterisation) is presented at the
  G-FREEZE review. No retuning was applied after observing results.
