## Methods

Matrix fixed per the frozen protocol; the plan's "2 search budgets" is
explicitly collapsed: protocol v1.0 froze ONE measured self-play budget
(128/32, from H2 profiling + H4 pilot, journals
H2-2026-09-09-throughput-profile-01 / H4-2026-09-10-pilot-01 — not the
plan's 64/128 placeholders) and D-019 pinned ONE evaluation budget (400
sims); the two comparison views are the two budget READINGS of plan
ch. 6 (same-examples, same-wall-clock), pre-registered in the matrix
with a measured, never-score-driven T* rule. Openings: 250 unique legal
4-ply openings from a seeded random walk over engine validmoves.
Harness: arena extended with `--openings-file`, `--records` (per-game
CSV: opening id, colour, score, truncation, plies, outcome, run label)
and analysis modes (summary / schedule / paired / seeds-bootstrap with
seed as the resampling unit). Self-play generator extended with
`--graph` (generic worker over both evaluators) and probed.

## Raw results + uncertainty

- **Harness (task 3):** two arena runs with different agents AND
  different arena seeds against the openings file produce byte-identical
  (opening, colour) schedules (hash 8cd84b6564440666); records
  round-trip through all analysis modes (summary, schedule, paired —
  paired diff computed over 10 shared openings on the smoke data).
- **Graph-arm self-play, first end-to-end measurement:** 8 games,
  128/32, 4 threads, 2-epoch smoke net: 281 s → 35 s/game (7/8 games at
  the 300-ply cap). Per-ply cost vs the grid's gen-0 measurement:
  ≈2.4× against the graph arm (500 vs 212 thread-ms/ply) — larger than
  the 1.4× pure-inference ratio (per-eval graph building + no CoreML).
- **Campaign sizing (measured-derived, proposal):** per run of
  10 generations × 500 games — grid ≈11 h (self-play 8.3 h at an
  improving 12→3 s/game, training ≈0.3 h, evals ≈2.7 h), graph ≈26 h
  (2.4× self-play, 1.4× eval). Three seeds per arm:
  **total ≈111 h ≈ 4.6 days continuous; disk ≈3–5 GB** (vs 164 GB free;
  20 GB guard). Reduced options: 7×400 ≈ 2.7 days; 5×300 ≈ 1.7 days.

## Limits / confounders

Graph self-play cost measured with a weak net (near-all-truncated
games); trained-net cost extrapolated by the grid arm's observed 12→3
improvement — the pilot (task 4) tightens this. Sizing assumes
sequential runs on this one machine.
