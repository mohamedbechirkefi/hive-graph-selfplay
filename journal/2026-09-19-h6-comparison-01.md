## Methods

Exactly the frozen protocol: 2 capacity-matched arms (1.44M vs 1.47M,
+2.1% reported) × 3 seeds × 10 generations × 500 games at 128/32 sims;
identical rules, search, decoder, records, training conventions; both
budget readings from one campaign — same-examples at the generation-10
checkpoints, same-wall-clock at each run's last checkpoint ≤ T* = 18.77 h
(grid: gen009/gen009/gen008; graph: gen003/gen004/gen004). Evaluation:
100 paired colour-swapped games per opponent on the frozen openings,
pinned settings; truncation a separate outcome throughout. Analysis:
per-(seed, opponent) aggregation, bootstrap over seeds (10,000 resamples),
no game pooled as i.i.d. Tables regenerate via `scripts/make_results.py`;
figures via `scripts/make_figures.py`.

## Limits / confounders

3 seeds per arm (the matrix's 5-seed option unexercised); one machine and
its provider asymmetry (reported, charged honestly in reading 2); one
graph architecture of one capacity — H1 is rejected for THIS simple
relational MP net at THIS budget, not for graph representations at large;
10 generations is early-regime self-play (all scores vs strong baselines
remain low); opening set fixed (frozen) and base game only.

## Interpretation

**H1 is REJECTED under the pre-registered rule.** Under both budget
readings the graph arm shows no seed-consistent advantage (it is behind
on B-RND and B-HEU for every seed-mean, level-at-best on B-MCTS), and
every interval excludes a meaningful graph advantage (largest upper
bound +0.035). Per protocol §1 this outcome is published as-is: **at
this budget, on base-game Hive, the graph representation did not help —
it scored lower against two of three frozen opponents under both
readings, at 2× the wall-clock cost.** Mechanistically, the clearest
signal is the graph arm's failure to convert won positions (20–57%
truncation vs random; fig4-F1), consistent with weaker local-tactical
sharpness — the direction Keller et al.'s Hex asymmetry predicted
(GNN long-range vs CNN local), now observed under matched AlphaZero-style
self-play in Hive.
