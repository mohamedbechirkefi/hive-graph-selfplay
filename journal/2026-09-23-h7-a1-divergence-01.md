## Methods

Exactly the A1 config; runs completed without process failure. Alarm
raised at monitoring: all three seeds' final evaluations were
IDENTICAL to the game count (e.g. 0/0/100 vs B-HEU; 2W/45D + 53%
truncation vs B-RND) — independent seeded trainings cannot agree to
that precision. Diagnosis followed the mandated order and landed on the
nets themselves: distinct checkpoint hashes, but every forward pass
returns **NaN** for policy and value, in all three seeds, already at
the FIRST checkpoint (gen000, epoch 0–1).

## Raw results + uncertainty

- Training **diverged to NaN in generation 0 for 3/3 seeds** and every
  later generation self-played and "trained" on NaN outputs. Log
  signature: `policy top-1 100.0%, value acc 0.0%` from gen001 onward.
- Wall-clock 7.7–8.0 h vs the ≈36 h reference: NaN priors degrade the
  eval/self-play search to short degenerate games (~42 plies mean, 0
  truncations, 0 resignations — NaN never crosses the resign
  threshold). The configured budget (games, sims) held; the wall-clock
  deviation is a divergence symptom, reported per task 2.
- **The final-eval score tables for A1 are NOT strength measurements**
  (a NaN policy plays a deterministic degenerate game independent of
  its weights — hence the identical seeds). They are recorded raw under
  `data/runs/cmp-graph-untyped-*/eval/` and excluded from H-T3 as
  scores; the H-T3 cell for A1 reads "training diverged (3/3 seeds)".

## Failures

The divergence itself (kept, per invariant 5 — this IS the result).
Tooling gap fixed: the training loop had no non-finite guard and the
loss print cadence (every 100 steps) never fired on ~20-step epochs, so
NaN passed silently for 10 generations; both training loops now HALT
loudly on non-finite loss (guard added 2026-09-23; behaviour of healthy
runs unchanged — A2's remaining runs inherit the guard, its s1 run is
verified finite at gen007).

## Limits / confounders

The mechanism attribution (shared edge matrix accumulates the summed
gradient of six neighbour terms ≈ 6× per-matrix gradient scale of the
typed variant at identical lr/momentum) is a well-grounded hypothesis,
not a measured decomposition; no lr sweep was run because changing lr
would make A1 a two-component diff.

## Interpretation

**A1's answer at parity: removing geometric edge typing removes
training stability — the naive-adjacency variant cannot be trained at
all under the full method's settings (NaN in generation 0, 3/3
seeds).** Per plan ch. 6's attribution rule this is stated exactly:
typed edges contribute AT MINIMUM optimization stability; nothing about
their strength contribution can be measured at parity because the
variant never trains. This sharpens protocol §1's premise: "a naive
adjacency graph may not suffice" — under these conditions it does not
even optimize. A supplementary NON-parity run (reduced lr or gradient
clipping, explicitly labeled a two-component diff and never attributed
to the graph alone) is possible if the human wants a trainable-A1
datapoint; it is not required for H-T3.

## Decision

**continue** — A1 journaled as diverged; A2 (healthy) keeps running;
supplementary-A1 question parked for the human.
