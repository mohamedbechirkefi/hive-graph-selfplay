≈0.00 ± 0.17 (B-RND), −0.01 ± 0.04 (B-HEU), −0.01 ± 0.03 (B-MCTS) — no

stability), H-T3 reads: of the graph arm's two distinctive components,
one is load-bearing for trainability itself and the other is
dispensable at this scale. Per plan ch. 6, nothing here is attributed
