## Methods

Ten runs, sequential, interleaved grid-11, graph-11, …, graph-15,
launched 2026-10-13 15:02:34 CET by `nohup caffeinate -i bash
scripts/campaign2.sh` (shell PID 88604, caffeinate PID 88607, log
`data/runs/campaign2.log`). Each run: ten generations of 500 self-play
games (128/32 simulations, Study 1 settings), training 2 epochs per
generation with warm start from the previous generation's checkpoint
and the shards of the last three generations, export, intermediate
evaluations at indices 4 and 7 (20 paired games per opponent) and the
final evaluation at index 9 (100 paired games per opponent). Nothing
