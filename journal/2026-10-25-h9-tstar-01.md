## Methods

Protocol v2 §4 fixes the rule: T*' is the median of the total training
wall-clock of the five Study 2 grid runs, each total being the sum of
the ten per-generation entries of the run's `wallclock.json`
(self-play, training and export; evaluations excluded), computed
exactly from the clock files once all five grid runs exist and before
any cross-arm number is assembled, never recomputed. A run's cutoff
checkpoint is the last generation whose cumulative clock is at or below
T*' (inclusive); where it is the final checkpoint (gen009), the final
evaluation is reused. The computation below was done at 06:28 CET on
2026-10-25, minutes after the fifth grid run (cmp2-grid-s15) completed
and while the last run (cmp2-graph-s15) was in its first self-play
generation; `scripts/make_results.py --study 2` implements the same
rule (`_tstar_hours`, `tstar_checkpoint`) and its value was checked
equal to the hand computation. Only clock files were opened.
